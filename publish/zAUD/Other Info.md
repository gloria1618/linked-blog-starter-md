Other info
- non/fin info (other than FS+aud report) incl in entity's annual report
- incl
	- report by mgmt or those charged w governance on ops
	- fin summary/highlight
	- employment data
	- fin ratios
	- selected quarterly data
- doesnt incl
	- press releases, cover letters accompanying doc containing audited FS+aud reports
	- info contained in analyst briefings
	- info contained on entity's website
- Audtr Resp for Other Info
	- audtr resp when report incl in doc that contains other info?
		- read other info
		- consider any M inconsistency bw other info vs aud FS
		- respond accordingly by det if aud FS or toher info needs revision
		- audtr should request mgmt to correct material inconsistency
- Material Inconsistency
	- upon id, action depends on what info req revision
	- aud FS req revision, mgmt refuses
		- modify opinion
	- other info req revision, mgmt refuses
		- audtr comms matter to those charged w governance 
		- consider implications for report
		- withhold report use
		- withdraw from eng, consult w legal counsel
- Material Misstmt
	- other info may incl MM of fact that's unrelated to FS data
	- ex
		- other info says company intro new products X, Y
	- if auditor becomes aware of MM of fact, discuss w mgmt
	- mgmt refuses to take corrective action
		- request mgmt consult w qualified 3rd party like legal counsel
			- if after consult audtr still believes MM of fact that mgmt refuses to fix
				- notify those charged w governance
