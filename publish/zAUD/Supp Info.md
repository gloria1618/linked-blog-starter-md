Req Supplementary Info 
- info that a designated acct stndrd setter (eg GASB, SEC) req to accompany basic FS
- gen, audtr opinion on FS *does not cover* req supp info
- audtr resp
	- perform limited proced on req supp info 
- Nonissuers
	- auditr adds sep section to report on FS: "Req Supp Info" to explain following circum as applicable
		- No issues w req supp info
			- req supp info incl, audtr applied req procedures
		- Issues w req supp info
			- all/some of req supp info omitted
			- some req supp info missing, some presented in accordance w prescribed guidelines
			- audtr id material departures from prescribed guidelines
			- audtr cant complete req proc or unresolved doubts abt conformance of req supp info
		- sepp section states req supp info is *resp of mgmt* & audtr *doesnt express opinion* on such info
	- for nonissuers, when req supp info is req to be presented, sep section added to aud report, regardless of if issues exist w info
- Issuers
	- PCAOB doesnt req audtr to add expl para to audited FS or refer to supp info unless one of following applicable
		- Issues w req supp info:
			- req info omitted
			- material departures from prescribed guidelines
			- auditor unable to complete prescribed procedures
			- unresolved doubts abt conformance of req supp info
