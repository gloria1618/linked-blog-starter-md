Overview of Qlty Mgmt for Nonissuer audtr
- AICPA Code of Prof Conduct req firms prov audit, attestation, acct/rev services to adopt SOQM 
- SOQM consists of policies/proced
	- dnI, maintained to ensure firm complies w prof stdrds, approp legal/reg req
	- reports issued are approp in circum
- SQMS
	- Stmts on Quality Mgmt Stndrds SQMS
	- ASB issued to prov guidance on managing eng quality
		- at least annually, firm eval SOQM + conclude if it prov firm w reasonable assurance that qlty mgmt obj achieved

Responsibility for SOQM
- prof judgement, accountability 2 key aspects of strong SOQM
- firm assigns resp for
	- ultimate resp for SOQM
		- typ assigned to CEO, managing partner, firm's mgmt board of partners
	- operational resp for SOQM
	- operational resp for specific aspects of SOQM
		- like compliance w ethics, indep req, compliance w monitoring/remediation process
- indiv assigned resp for SOQM or underlying aspects must have approp knowledge, experience, influence, authority 
	- thorough understanding of role
	- indiv w resp for underlying parts should have direct comms line to indivs w overall resp for SOQM 
- parts of SOQM
	- firm est strong SOQM to ensure personnel
		- comply w prof stndrds
		- meet legal/regulatory req
		- issue approp reports
	- req qlty mgmt to be responsive to risks, diff circum for firm+eng
	- 8 parts
		1. Firm's risk assessment process
			- SOQM incl DnI of risk assessment process
				- to proactively meet qlty mgmt obj, firm's SOQM must be responsive to risks, diff circum for firm+eng
				- risk based approach applied when DnIing+op SOQM and consider both nature/circum of firm/eng performed by firm
			- risk based approach achieved by
				- est qlty obj
					- qlty obj set by firm rep desired outcomes related to each part of SOQM. firm req to est certain specified qlty obj but must also consider need to est additional obj that may be relevant to mtg overall obj of SOQM
				- id, assess risks of qlty obj (qlty risks)
					- qlty risks must have reasonable possibility of occurring, indiv/combo w other risks, threaten achievement of an est qlty obj. to effectively id qlty risks, firm considers both factors related to firm+eng
						- firm specific qlty risk considerations incl complexity/op chara, business model, mgmt/leadership style, avail firm resources, applicable law/reg/prof stndrds of firm
						- eng specific qlty risk considerations incl both types of eng performed and entity types
				- DnIing responses to address qlty risks
					- SQMS (stmts on qlty mgmt stdrds) est specific responses to incl in firm's response to qlty risks, but judgement should be applied in det additional responses necessary to achieve obj of SOQM. pol/proced est to id info indicating additional/mod qlty risks
					- firm response to qlty risks incl
						- est pol/proced to id/address compliance threats w ethical req
						- est policies/proced to id/report breaches of ethical req/respond in timely manner
						- at least annually, obtain documented confirmation of compliance w independence req by all req personnel 
						- est process for receive, investigate, resolve complaints abt failures to comply w firm/prof stndrds + app legal/reg req
						- est pol/proced abt info received after accept/cont client relationship that woulda cased it to decline eng
						- est pol for when firm obligated by law/reg to accept client relation/eng
						- est pol/proced when comms w external parties abt firm's SOQM is approp, awa approp type/form of comms 
						- est pol to address eng qlty reviews
		2. Governance, leadership
			- tone at top, but all personnel should. qlty obj to gov/leadership incl
				- demonstrate firm commitment to qlty by est culture that recog, reinforces firms role in serving public interest, importance of prof ethics, shared resp of all personnel for qlty performance, importance of firm's strategic decisions
				- leadership resp/accountable for qlty
				- leadership demonstrates qlty commitment
				- org structure (role, resp, auth) approp to enable op of firms SOQM
				- resources planned for/allocated in manner consistent w firms qlty commitment
		3. Relevant ethical req
			- stndrds est fundamental ethical principles governing way firm+personnel do prof resp. incl resp, public interest, integrity, objectivity+indep, due care, scope+nature of services. firm should est qlty obj related to ethical req like
				- firm+personnel understand+adhere to relevant ethical req+fulfill resp w respect to violations
				- others (eg network firms, service providers) subj to ethical req must understand/adhere to est req
			- firm SOQM may be more specific or in addition to ethical req est by stndrds
				- ex: firm may set lower threshold/fully prohibit client gifts
