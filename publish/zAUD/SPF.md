Reporting on Special Purpose Frameworks

- audtr eval FS based on framework selected by mgmt
- nonissuers can prep FS using SPF
- SPF
	- FRF other than GAAP that is one of following bases of acct:
	- Cash Basis
		- to record cash receipts/disbursements, certain mod of cash basis (eg deprec)
	- Tax basis
		- used to file its income tax return
	- Regulatory Basis
		- used to comply w req or fin reporting provisions of a regulatory agency having jurisdiction over reporting entity
	- Contractual Basis
		- used to comply w an agrmt bw entity and 1+ 3rd parties (other than audtr)
	- Other Basis
		- used to define logical, reasonable crit est that's applied to all material items appearing in FS
