### 5 Step Approach
ISTAR
1. ID contract w customer
	- 5 steps applied only when contract meets all crit
		- all parties approved contract, committed to perform obligations
		- each party rights regarding goods identified
		- pmt terms identified
		- commercial substance 
			- fut CF (amt, risk, timing) expected to ▲ bc contract
		- probable entity will collect subs all consideration due 
	- assess at inception, reassess if sig changes occur
	- if not met at inception, regular reassessments follow
	- crit not met, consideration paid: recog rev if
		- consideration nonrefundable
		- either no remaining obligations to transfer goods or contract terminated
		- ...if not revenue, then L (unearned rev)
	- combo of contracts
		- 2+ contracts entered into w same customer/related parties at/near same time
		- combine, accounted for as single contract if either
			- contracts negotiated as package w single commercial obj
			- consideration for 1 contract tied to performance price of another
			- goods promised rep single performance obligation
	- contract modification
		- rep ▲ in price/scope approved by both parties
		- when modification occurs, either treated as
			- new contract
				- scope increases due to addition of distinct goods, and
				- price ↑ approp reflects standalone selling prices of additions
			- mod of existing
				- nondistinct goods
				- adj to revenue to reflect transaction price ▲
2. ID Sep performance obligations
	- performance obligation = promise to transfer good
	- transfer distinct indiv or series of goods
	- if transfer promise not distinct from other goods, all combined into one single performance obligation
	- distinct crit
		1. transfer promise sep identifiable from other good.service in contract
			- entity doesn't integrate good w other contract goods
			- goods dont customize/modify another contract good
			- good doesn't depend/relate to other goods in contract
		2. customer can benefit from good independently or when combined w customers avail resources
	- not sep identifiable
		- highly interrelated/dependent
		- entity prov sig service of intergrating good w other goods promised in contract into a good bundle that rep combined output contracted for by customer

3. det Transaction price
	- det based on considering effects of
		- variable consideration (any constraining est)
			- est by
				- taking range of possible amts, and
				- use better predictor
					- expected value (sums probability weighted amts)
					- most likely amt
			- only incl in price if probable sig revenue reversal wont be req once any uncertainty tied to consideration resolved
		- sig financing (if applicable)
			- time value of $ should be adj to transaction price if pmt timing per contract provides either party w sig benefit regarding financing transfer of goods
			- rev recog based on price that woulda been paid in cash at transfer time
			- discounting unnecessary if time bw transfer + pmt is <1yr
		- noncash considerations 
			- FV at contract inception
		- any consideration payable to customer (if applicable)
			- treat as reduction in transaction price and rev recog

4. Allocate transaction price 
	- mult performance obligation, alloc transaction price to each sep perf obl based on consideration amt expected for satisfying each unique obligation
	- det at inception stand alone selling price (applicable discount, variable consideration) of each distinct good underlying each perf oblig
	- stand alone selling price
		- once det for each obligation, tot price alloc in proportion 
	- discounts
		- exists when sum of stand alone prices > tot consideration
		- alloc proportionally to all obligations
	- variable consideration may be attributable to
		- entire contract
		- indiv performance obligations w.in contract
		- distinct goods w.in single perf oblig
	- transaction price ▲
		- if ▲ after inception, ▲ alloc to perf oblig on same basis used at inception
		- ▲ in stand alone selling prices after inception not realloc

	- when/as entity satisfies each performance obligation
	- when customer has control
	- control
		- ability to obtain benefits from, direct usage of , A while preventing other entities from obtaining benefits n directing usage
	- satisfied over time
		- any of crit met
			- annual service contract
				- performance create/enhance A that customer controls as A created/enhanced (wip)
			- subscription service
				- customer stim receives/consumes bene as entity performs
			- not basic inventory
				- performance doesnt create A w alt use to entity, entity has enforceable right to receive pmt for performance complete to date
		- be able to reasonably measure progress
			- output methods
				- rev recog based on value to customer of what's transferred relative to remaining promised
				- ex: units produced/delivered, time elapsed, milestones achieved, surveys of performance completed to dat, appraisals fo results achieved

- Warranties
	- acct depends on if customer has option to purchase warranty sep
