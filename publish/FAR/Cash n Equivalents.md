- Cash incl
	- currency
	- demand deposits w banks/other fin instit
- Cash Equivalents incl
	- ST, highly liquid investments that're both
		- readily convertible to cash
		- have og maturity of =<90dys from purchase date when acquired by entity
- Ex
	- coin, currency on hand (incl petty cash)
	- checking, savings accts
	- money mkt funds
	- deposits held as compensating blnces against borrowing arrangements w lending instit that are *not* legally restricted
		- if legally restricted /=/ cash n equiv
	- Negotiable paper
		- bank/traveler's/cashier's checks, money orders, bank drafts
		- commercial paper, treasury bills
		- certificates of deposit (og maturity <=90dys)
- NOT Cash/Equiv
	- time certificates of deposit (if og maturity >90dys)
	- legally restricted deposits held as compensating blnces against borrowing arrangements w a lending instit
		- compensating blnce - part of cash.equiv contractually restricted bc of fin arrangements w credit instit, restriction lowers cost of borrowing
- Restricted
	- set aside for specific use/purpose (eg purpose of PPE)
	- nature, amt, timing of restrictions disclosed in footnotes
		- restriction assoc w current A/L
			- classify as current A, but separate from unrestricted
		- restriction assoc w noncurrent A/L
			- classify as noncurrent A, but separate from either Investments or Other A section
	- ex
		- if any part of cash.equiv restricted by mgmt, report as restricted cash and as current/LT A (depending on anticipated disbursement date)
		- some industries (ex public utilities) reports cash.equiv amts as last A on BS bc report A in inverse order of liquidity
- Unrestricted
	- used for all current op
