### Purchased Software, Cloud Computing Arrangements
- Purchased Software
	- finite life, identifiable, intangible A
	- BS as InA at purchase price
	- amort over shorter of
		- legal life (contractual term)
		- eco life
- Cloud Computing
	- finite life, identifiable, intangible A
	- CCArrangement
		- involves paying vendor fee in exchange for right to use software over internet
		- vendor resp for hosting software on its infra
		- 3 phases
			1. Preliminary Proj Phase
				- involv det sys req for software
				- costs incurred expensed as incurred
			2. Application Development Phase
				- involv work to customize/▲ infra/config
				- capitalize: implementation costs
					- software, software licensing, 3rdparty software dev fees, external materials, coding/testing fees
					- recorded as InA
					- expensed over term of hosting arrangement
					- amort begins when any module/part of CCA ready for use
				- expense:
					- training, manual data conversion, maintenance/support costs
			3. Post Implementation Phase
				- begins when software placed in service and incl maintenance, additional training, enhancements, upgrades
				- costs incurred expensed as incurred
	- ![[Pasted image 20260805021337.png]]
- Purchased Software, CCA Impairment Test
	- using 2 step impairment test
	- if sum of undiscounted fut CF < CV
		- impaired
		- impairment loss = FV - CV

### Franchisee Acct
- finite, InA, identifiable
- initial franchise costs capitalized
	- PV of amt (to be) paid by franchisee
	- cash paid + PV of any issued note + FMV issued stock
		- recorded as InA o BS
		- amort over expected life of franchise
- ongoing/cont franchise costs expensed as incurred
	- franchise royalties
		- received for ongoing services provided by franchisor to franchisee 
		- franchisee expense, franchisor revenue in period incurred
	- calc based on % of franchise revenues
	- services
		- mgmt training
		- promotion
		- legal assistance
