### Admission of Partner
- new partner may be admitted by
	- purchasing exisiting partnership interest
		- partner, w consent of all others, may sell partnership interest to new partner
		- pmt directly to selling partner
			- no entries made on partnership bks, except ▲ name on capital acct
			- A/L/tot capital not affected
	- investing additional capital into partnership
- Partnership contributions recording
	- A: FV
	- L: assumed PV

### Partner Withdrawal
2 methods
- Bonus Method
	- diff bw blnce of withdrawing partner's capital acct n amt person paid = bonus amt
	- 'bonus' alloc among remaining partners' capital acct accord w reamining profit/loss ratio
	- altho partnership's identifiable A may be revalued to FV at withdrawal date, any goodwill implied by excess pmt to retiring partner isn't recorded
	- ![[Pasted image 20260812031243.png]]
- Goodwill Method
	- partners may elect to record implied GW in partnership based on pmt to withdrawing partner
	- amt of implied GW allocated to all of partners in accord w profit/loss ratios
	- after allocation of implied GW of partnership, blnce in withdrawing partner's capital acct = amt person is to receive in final settlement of interest
	- ![[Pasted image 20260812031303.png]]

### Partnership Liquidation
- GAAP, partnership liquidation involves
	- disposing A, collecting cash
	- paying off all L to creditors
	- dist remainder to partners
- Order of Dist A
	- 1st creditors 
		- creditors, incl partners who're creditors
	- 2nd Partners' capital acct liquidated
		- right of offset bw partner's loans to/from partnership and that partner's capital blnces gen exists in liquidation
	- if deficiency, remaining partners absorb deficiency accord to remaining profit/loss ratios
- Losses Considered
	- in liquidation, all possible losses must be provided for before any dist to partners
	- losses charged to partners in accord w agrmt
		- if not agrmt, equally shared losses
- Converting Noncash A
	- gen procedure is all noncash A converted into cash by selling n recog g/l on sale
	- cash used to pay L
	- liquidation of partnership A may result in
		- g/l on realization
		- l on realization resulting in capital deficiency
- Capital Deficiency
	- dr blnce in partner's capital acct (equity acct), indicates partnership has claim against partner for deficiency
		- if partner w capital deficiency has loan acct
			- means partnership has L to partner
			- partner has legal right to offset, may use loan acct to satisfy capital defic
		- if defic still exists
			- remaining partners must absorb deficiency accord to remaining profit/loss ratios
- Partnership Liquidation Sch
	- obj of sch is to dist cash as it becomes avail, to partners
	- important that no partner over/underpaid
